# Handoff: from one person or AI to the next

Whoever picks up the work (Erik, Claude, Grok, Hermes, the social engine) gets:
- This folder. Nothing else is the record.
- `_context/` current, with every open question in `decisions.md` and who answers it.
- The week's brief and its draft folder.
Before handing off, add a HANDOFF row to `change-log.md`: from, to, what moved.
A tool that keeps its own memory still writes what it learned back here.
