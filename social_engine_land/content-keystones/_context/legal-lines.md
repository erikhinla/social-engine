# Legal lines and claims

Owner: Erik H Bush. Use word for word.

| Use | Line |
|---|---|
| Any worked cost example | Example: 6 people at $90,000, each losing 5 hours a week to finding, checking and redoing work. That's $67,500 a year. |
| Deck screenshots | Source-deck descriptions, not a live offer or measured client result. |
| Invented scenes in ads | No line needed on the post, but never attach a company name, a real client, or a percentage to them. |

## Never claim

- A measured client result. We don't have one published yet.
- A percentage or a survey number without a source in this file.
- A FACT price, anywhere.
- That PROOF or BACK "fixes" or "guarantees" anything. PROOF shows where work stalls. BACK builds the files and rules. The team puts them in place.
