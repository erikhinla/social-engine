# AI-CONTEXT: read this before writing anything for TransformBy10X

Any assistant (Claude, Grok, Hermes, the social engine) reads these files in this order before it writes a word:

1. `brand.md`: the thesis, the voice, the words we use and the ones we don't.
2. `channels.md`: what each channel is for and the format it takes.
3. `hooks.md`: the approved hooks. Use these before inventing new ones.
4. `mandatories.md`: links, handles, prices and design values. Current column only.
5. `legal-lines.md`: what we may claim. Word for word.
6. `rights-and-licenses.md`: which footage, images and audio may be used where.
7. `decisions.md` and `change-log.md`: what's been decided recently. Newer rows win.
8. `learnings.md`: what the numbers taught us. Follow it over your own instinct.

## Rules that never bend without Erik

- Never post, schedule, boost or spend. Drafts only. Erik approves each piece.
- Never print a FACT price. BACK never mentions FACT.
- No em dashes. No emojis in copy. Headlines in solid ink or white, never gradient.
- A hook must show AI output outrunning a handoff. It never blames a person.
- Examples and scenes are labeled as examples. Never present an invented number as a client result.
- If a fact you need isn't in these files, write `OPEN:` and the question in the draft. Don't guess.
