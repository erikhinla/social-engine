# Brand: what we believe and how we say it

Owner: Erik H Bush. Changes follow `protocols/change-protocol.md`.

## The thesis

**Your team adopted AI. Your infrastructure didn't.**

AI makes more work possible than traditional BizOps can carry. Output now moves at AI speed, but briefs, feedback, approvals and decisions still move at human-handoff speed. The context drops in the gap. Adoption isn't the problem. The missing infrastructure is.

Tagline: **Infrastructure before intelligence.**

## Message house

| Floor | What it says | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| Roof | Your team adopted AI. Your infrastructure didn't. | Every campaign, erikhbush.com hero |
| Pillar 1: Recognition | Scenes the reader has lived: two versions, a legal line that didn't travel, the brief re-explained on Sunday. | Most posts |
| Pillar 2: Pressure | Shops built after AI quote faster. Not better people, better structure. | One in four posts |
| Pillar 3: Proof | PROOF names where your work stalls in 15 answers. BACK builds the structure from them. | Every CTA |
| Foundation | Erik ran agency operations for twenty years. He's watched every handoff break. | Founder channels |

## Voice

- Plain, specific, a little dry. The person who's seen it all and isn't shocked by it.
- Scenes over statistics. "The editor's marking up v2. The client approved v3." beats "73% of teams..."
- Short sentences. Second person for the company, first person for Erik.
- Never cheerlead AI. Never fear-monger about it. AI is fine. The plumbing isn't.

## Words

Use: PROOF, BACK, FACT (product names only), workflow, handoff, context, infrastructure, speed, version, brief, sign-off.
Avoid: synergy, leverage, unlock, revolutionize, game-changer, supercharge, 10x your (as a verb), digital fog (retired campaign language), FLOW Agent, Mr. Nobody, BizBuilders.

## The hook test

Every hook answers yes to both:
1. Does it show AI output moving faster than a handoff?
2. Would the person in the scene read it and feel seen, not blamed?

Retired for failing the test: the "3-day turnaround" hook (it blames a person).

The proof sheet: paper, ink, one magenta mark-up color, crop marks, stamps. Inter Tight for headlines, IBM Plex Mono for labels. Values are in `mandatories.md`.
