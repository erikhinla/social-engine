# Channels: what each one is for

Make one idea a week, then cut it per channel. A channel gets a format that fits the way people use it there, not a copy of the X post.

| Channel id | Account | Its job | Format that wins there | Link | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| x-tbtx | X @transformby10x | Recognition in one breath. The scene, the turn, the question. | Text under 280, one proof-sheet still on 1 of 3 | First reply, not the post (X shows link posts to fewer people) | 3 a week |
| x-erik | X @ErikHBush | Erik's view from the floor. First person, a little dry. | Text only, sometimes a Prompting Circumstance screenshot | First reply | 3 a week, the morning before the matching company post |
| li-erik | LinkedIn, Erik Howerbush | Where buyers read. The long version of the scene with the twenty years behind it. | 900 to 1,300 characters with white space, or a PDF document carousel | First comment | 2 a week, Tue and Thu morning |

## Rules per channel

- **X:** no hashtags. The question "Is your workflow built for AI speed?" closes at most one company post a week, so it stays fresh.
- **LinkedIn personal:** first line has to work alone, because it's all that shows before "see more". No outside link in the post body.
- **Instagram and YouTube Shorts:** the first frame carries the headline in ink on paper so it reads with the sound off. Silent unless the music is picked from the platform's licensed library in-app.
- **Every link:** carries `?s=<post id>` (see `mandatories.md`).
